Our story

A family legacy of clean

JV Cleaning LLC has provided professional cleaning services since 2002, built on a simple belief: a clean environment is more than appearance. It creates spaces that feel welcoming, work better and leave a strong first impression.

The business began with Alicia Domínguez, the family matriarch, whose commitment to excellence set the company's values: understand each client's needs, pay attention to detail and provide dependable service.

Today that legacy continues under the leadership of Johanna Ríos and Samuel Mendoza. The company remains family-owned, combining innovation and professionalism with the personalized care that started it all.

Common questions

FAQ

Do you handle both interior and exterior cleaning?

Yes. JV Cleaning covers interior commercial cleaning, and our Rhino Sweepers division handles parking-lot sweeping and exterior services, so one team can care for the whole property.

Do you work outside normal business hours?

Flexible service windows may be available. Tell us your operating hours and we will find the least disruptive time.

Do you offer maintenance contracts or one-time services?

Both. We offer one-time services and recurring plans on a weekly, biweekly or monthly schedule.

What do you need to give me a quote?

Tell us the type of property, approximate area, the services you need and the desired frequency. We use those details to prepare a quote.

How is the final price determined?

Pricing depends on property size, surface conditions, scope, access and frequency. We confirm the full scope before any work begins.
